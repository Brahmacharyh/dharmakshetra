This Privacy Policy explains how DHARMAKSHETRA handles information in connection with the use of the application and its currently available features.

Last Updated: November 8, 2026

01

About This Policy

DHARMAKSHETRA is an independent application dedicated to the exploration, understanding, and preservation of Sanatana Dharma.

This Privacy Policy describes how information associated with your use of DHARMAKSHETRA may be collected, stored, and used through the application's currently implemented features.

02

Account Information

DHARMAKSHETRA includes account and profile functionality. Information provided during the creation or use of an account may be processed to provide and maintain the relevant account and application features.

The specific information requested within the application may depend upon the version and features currently available.

03

Firebase Services

DHARMAKSHETRA currently uses Firebase services provided by Google to support certain application functionality.

The Firebase services currently implemented include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore

Firebase Authentication is used to support account-related functionality. Cloud Firestore may be used to store and manage information required for the application's implemented features.

04

How Information Is Used

Information associated with the application's implemented features may be used for purposes including:

  • Creating and maintaining user accounts
  • Providing account and profile functionality
  • Supporting the operation of application features
  • Maintaining and improving the application's functionality
  • Protecting the proper operation and security of the application
05

Data Storage

Information required for supported account and application features may be stored using Cloud Firestore or other services required for the proper operation of the application.

The information stored depends on the features you use and the information required by those features.

06

Data Security

DHARMAKSHETRA seeks to use reasonable technical measures and appropriate service configurations to support the protection of information handled through the application.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

07

Third-Party Services

Firebase services used by DHARMAKSHETRA are provided by Google. The handling of information by those services may also be subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

08

Future Features

DHARMAKSHETRA is under continuing development. New features may be introduced in future versions of the application.

If a future feature changes the way information is collected, stored, or processed, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect those changes.

09

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the application, its features, services, or information-handling practices.

The latest version will be made available through the official DHARMAKSHETRA website or another appropriate official channel.

10

Contact

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please use the official feedback or contact options made available by DHARMAKSHETRA.
