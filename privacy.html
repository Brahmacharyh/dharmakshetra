Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in connection with the use of DHARMAKSHETRA and its currently available features.

LAST UPDATED · NOVEMBER 8, 2026
01

About this policy

DHARMAKSHETRA is an independent application dedicated to the exploration, understanding, and preservation of Sanatana Dharma.

This Privacy Policy describes how information associated with your use of DHARMAKSHETRA may be handled through the application's currently available features.

02

Account information

DHARMAKSHETRA includes account and profile functionality. Information provided during account creation or use may be processed to provide and maintain relevant application features.

The information requested may depend on the version and features currently available within the application.

03

Firebase services

DHARMAKSHETRA currently uses certain Firebase services provided by Google to support account-related and application functionality.

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore

These services may process or store information required for the operation of implemented account and application features.

04

How information is used

Information may be used where necessary to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts
  • Provide account and profile functionality
  • Support the operation of available features
  • Maintain and improve the application
  • Support security and proper operation
05

Data storage

Information required for supported application features may be stored using Cloud Firestore and other services necessary for the operation of DHARMAKSHETRA.

The information involved depends on the features you use and the information required by those features.

06

Data security

Reasonable technical measures and appropriate service configurations may be used to support the protection of information handled through the application.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

07

Third-party services

Firebase services used by DHARMAKSHETRA are provided by Google. The handling of information by those services may also be subject to Google's applicable policies and terms.

08

Future features

DHARMAKSHETRA is under continuing development. New features may be introduced in future versions.

If future features change the way information is collected, stored, or processed, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

09

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application, its services, features, or information handling practices.

The latest version will be made available through the official DHARMAKSHETRA website or another appropriate official channel.

CONTACT & QUESTIONS
