The Dharmakshetra standard

Reverence in spirit.
Rigor in understanding.

Sacred subjects deserve thoughtful treatment. Our approach values devotion while remaining attentive to sources, historical context, and the diversity of traditions.

01 / SOURCES

Grounded in references

Identify texts, traditions, and sources wherever possible. Do not present unsupported claims as established fact.

02 / CONTEXT

Respectful of diversity

Recognise different sampradāyas, regional customs, philosophical schools, and historical settings.

03 / CLARITY

Fact and interpretation

Distinguish textual statements, historical evidence, traditional belief, and scholarly interpretation.

04 / DIGNITY

Measured in expression

Present sacred matters with precision, balance, intellectual honesty, and respect.
