Our editorial commitment

Deep respect.
Clear thinking.

Faith and inquiry need not be opposites. We aim to approach Hindu traditions with sincerity while being precise about what sources say and what remains a matter of interpretation.

01 / SOURCE

Textual grounding

Identify the source or tradition behind a statement. Avoid invented quotations and unsupported certainty.

02 / CONTEXT

Historical awareness

Place ideas, practices and texts in their appropriate historical, regional and philosophical settings.

03 / DISTINCTION

Intellectual honesty

Separate textual evidence, traditional belief, historical fact and modern interpretation.

04 / RESPECT

Dignified expression

Represent diverse sampradāyas and viewpoints without mockery, sensationalism or needless conflict.
