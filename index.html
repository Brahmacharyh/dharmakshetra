A little more about the initiative and its purpose.

DHARMA KSHETRA is a digital initiative dedicated to the exploration, understanding and preservation of Sanatana Dharma and its traditional knowledge.
The Android application is currently under development. Official release and download information will be announced through this website.
The planned platform includes sacred scriptures, Sanskrit learning, translations, meanings and explanations supported by references.
The project aims to provide appropriate textual references and distinguish original scripture from translations and interpretations.
Official announcements and application release information will be shared through the official DHARMA KSHETRA communication channels.
