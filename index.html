The purpose

A thoughtful companion for the study of Sanatana Dharma.

Dharmakshetra is being developed to make scriptural learning more approachable without treating sacred knowledge as disposable content. Its aim is to bring texts, explanations and learning tools together in one considered experience.

01 / READ
अ

Engage with the texts

Explore scriptural passages in a reader designed to give the text room, clarity and attention.

02 / UNDERSTAND
ॐ

Study words and meaning

Examine Sanskrit vocabulary, transliteration, meanings and grammatical insights alongside the passage.

03 / PRESERVE
✧

Respect source and context

Keep references visible and approach translations and explanations with scholarly care.

Dharmakshetra principles

Knowledge deserves discipline.

The way sacred knowledge is presented matters. These principles guide the spirit in which the platform approaches learning and preservation.

I — SOURCE

Authenticity

Give priority to identifiable texts and traceable references.

II — STUDY

Discernment

Distinguish the source text from translation, commentary and interpretation.

III — CONTEXT

Historical care

Respect the setting, tradition and intellectual context of each work.

IV — DUTY

Preservation

Present inherited knowledge with responsibility and humility.

A simple beginning
