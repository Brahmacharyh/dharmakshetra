The purpose

A place where learning becomes a practice.

Dharmakshetra is conceived as more than a digital collection. It is a learning companion intended to help a seeker approach traditional knowledge gradually, attentively and with reverence.

Knowledge & inquiry
Our guiding principles

Knowledge deserves both reverence and discernment.

The name Dharmakshetra evokes a field of duty, inquiry and inner discipline. These principles shape the spirit in which learning is presented.

I

Śraddhā — reverent attention

Approach sacred literature with sincerity, patience and respect for the tradition from which it comes.

II

Vicāra — careful inquiry

Distinguish the source text, translation, commentary and personal interpretation. Do not confuse one with another.

III

Abhyāsa — disciplined practice

Let learning mature through regular study, thoughtful repetition and the gradual cultivation of memory.

IV

Ācāra — knowledge in conduct

Let understanding inform conduct, responsibility and the pursuit of a life ordered by Dharma.

“Not by hurried reading alone, but by reflection, practice and understanding does knowledge take root.”

A guiding thought of Dharmakshetra

A considered way to learn

From first encounter to confident remembrance.

The learning journey is designed around a simple progression: encounter the text, understand its meaning, practise it, and return to it.

01

02

03

Return to the passage for recitation and revision, allowing familiarity to develop into lasting knowledge.

A few clarifications

Questions of purpose and practice.

Some important distinctions about the role of a digital learning companion.

Is Dharmakshetra only a scripture-reading app?

No. Its broader purpose is to support learning: reading, understanding, recitation, remembrance and progressive study. The exact tools available depend on the current app release.

Does the app replace a Guru or traditional teacher?

No. A digital resource can assist personal study, but it does not replace the guidance of a qualified teacher, lineage-specific instruction or the living traditions of learning.

Will every scripture and mantra be included?

The library is intended to grow over time. Availability, translations and explanations should be considered according to the material actually published in each release.

Can beginners use it?

The learning approach is intended to welcome a beginner while also supporting sustained, more advanced study. Begin with accessible material and progress carefully.

The invitation

Let the study of Dharma become a lifelong discipline.

Read with humility. Question with sincerity. Practise with constancy. Allow knowledge to shape both understanding and conduct.
