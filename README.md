# DHARMAKSHETRA
Official DHARMAKSHETRA Android Application 
# About 
DHARMAKSHETRA is an independent Android application dedicated to the exploration, understanding, and preservation of Sanatana Dharma.
The project aims to bring aspects of Sanatana knowledge and tradition into a modern digital environment, making learning and exploration more accessible through technology.
DHARMAKSHETRA is an independent project and is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by any organisation, institution, temple, religious authority, or educational body, unless explicitly stated.

# Who Is This App For?
DHARMAKSHETRA is centred on Sanatana Dharma, while remaining open to all users who wish to explore and learn respectfully.
People from different backgrounds, cultures, languages, nationalities, and faiths are welcome.
The application does not discriminate against users on the basis of their faith or background.
Respect for the platform and for fellow users is expected from everyone.
